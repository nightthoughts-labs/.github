# Night Thoughts Labs

**Applied AI, tested for real.**

Night Thoughts Labs turns the experiments behind [nightthoughts.me](https://nightthoughts.me) into private, production-minded tools. Every product starts as a measured experiment: real data, held-out evaluation, documented failure modes, and a human in the loop where it matters.

## Products

### Medical Imaging Review Workflow
A local-first AI drafting workflow for chest radiographs. A deterministic readiness check prepares each study, a local model drafts the findings, and **nothing is exported until a qualified reviewer approves it**. Runs on your own hardware, so images never leave the site.

- Durable, resumable pipeline with full audit trail
- Doctor and operator review console
- Policy gates before and after the model

*Research demonstration. Not a medical device and not clinically validated.*
